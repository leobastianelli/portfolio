title: Victoriapp
role: Personal project

## Summary
An app that centralizes what a student needs from their department: class schedules, exam dates, per-subject WhatsApp groups, and notes organized by year. With a personal profile, a degree-progress calculator, and an admin panel so the student association can update everything without touching code.

## Technical
Next.js with Firebase (Auth and Firestore) as the backend, with role-based access between student and admin. The admin panel provides full CRUD for degree programs, subjects, exams, schedules, and institutional content.
