title: Onefam Community Portal
role: Full-stack developer

## Summary
A membership program for a European hostel chain. Guests subscribe, earn cashback on their bookings, and redeem it at check-in.

## Technical
Next.js on Vercel, Supabase for user data, Stripe customer-ID auth with automatic signup, three subscription tiers, a cashback ledger, and a Cloudbeds API integration to apply credit directly to the booking.
