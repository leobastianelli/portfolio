title: Púrpura Ceniza
role: Personal project

## Summary
My band's website. Full design and development.

## Technical
A static public site (plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no framework or build step), with a blog of individual posts served from a central content file. A custom admin panel handles login via Supabase Auth magic links, with data in Postgres (setlists, calendar, cash flow, repertoire, contacts) and audio files in Supabase Storage. It's an installable PWA. Continuous deployment on Vercel.
